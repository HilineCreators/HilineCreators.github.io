

# Hiline Creators — "Architecting Secure Intelligence"

## Design & Aesthetic
- **Deep dark background** (#0a0a0f range) with electric blue and neon green accent glows
- AI-themed visual elements: neural network patterns, circuit-board lines, glowing nodes, and abstract AI brain/shield graphics
- Subtle animated particle effects or floating geometric shapes evoking AI and cybersecurity
- Modern, sharp typography with glowing text effects on key headlines
- Smooth scroll navigation with fade-in animations on each section

## Navigation
- Sticky transparent navbar that becomes solid on scroll
- "Hiline Creators" logo/brand on the left
- Section links: Services, Team, Blog, Contact on the right
- Mobile hamburger menu
- Social icons (GitHub, blog) in the navbar

## Sections
